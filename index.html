Profile04
Profile

I don’t wait for deals. I build them.

Tech sales across web development, design and SEO. Raised in Kutch, based in Rajkot.

I’m Krishika Pandhi. I sell digital growth: websites that perform, brands that look the part, and SEO that brings in the right customers. I’m good at it because I care whether my clients win.

Sales isn’t my job title. It’s how I think. I read people fast, I say what I mean, and I don’t let a good opportunity go quiet.

I’m outgoing, lively and very focused. I’m also a night owl, which is why my best ideas usually show up after midnight.
